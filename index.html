    Quick answers.

    Still stuck? Ask us.

    Do you test pool water?

    Yes, at both stores. $9, or free with any pool product. Bring a bottle of pool water.

    Can I order online?

    Add items to your order list and send it. We confirm price and stock, then you collect and pay in store.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, free on Fridays for trade accounts around Mareeba and Atherton. Order by Thursday.

    How do I open a trade account?

    Pick up a form at either store, fill it in and hand it back.

    Do you sell to the public?

    Yes. Everyone is welcome.

    Can you fix my pool pump or chlorinator?

    Yes. We repair, replace and install pumps, chlorinators, filters and heaters across the Tablelands.

    Which Dolphin pool cleaner should I buy?

    Tell us your pool size and surface and we'll match one.

    I can't find a product on the website.

    We stock over 6,000 products. Add it as a custom line in your order list, or ring us.

    When are you open?

    Mareeba: weekdays 8:30 am to 5 pm. Atherton: weekdays 8 am to 5 pm. Both: Saturday 9 am to 12 noon. Closed Sundays and public holidays.

    Do you service pools?

    Yes, across the Atherton Tablelands, from $66. Regular visits, one-off cleans and green-pool recoveries.
