    Common questions

    Quick answers.

    Do you test pool water?

    Yes, at both stores. $9 a test, free when you buy any pool product. Bring a bottle of pool water.

    Can I order online?

    Add items to your order list on this page and send it to us. We confirm price and stock, then you collect in store. No online payment yet.

    Do you deliver?

    Yes, free on Fridays for trade account customers around Mareeba and Atherton. Order by Thursday.

    How do I open a trade account?

    Pick up a form at the counter of either store, fill it in, and hand it back. We set you up.

    Do you sell to the public?

    Yes. Everyone is welcome, trade or not.

    Can you fix my pool pump or chlorinator?

    Yes. We repair, replace and install pumps, chlorinators, filters and heaters anywhere on the Tablelands. Send us a photo or the model.

    Which Dolphin pool cleaner should I buy?

    Tell us your pool size and surface and we will match one. We stock the range and show you how they work.

    I can't find a product on the website.

    We list our best sellers here but stock over 6,000 products. Add it as a custom line in your order list, or ring us.

    When are you open?

    Mareeba: weekdays 8:30 am to 5 pm. Atherton: weekdays 8 am to 5 pm. Both: Saturday 9 am to 12 noon. Closed Sundays and public holidays.

    Do you service pools?

    Yes, across the Atherton Tablelands. Regular visits from $66, plus one-off cleans and green-pool recoveries. Send us your suburb and we will book you in.
